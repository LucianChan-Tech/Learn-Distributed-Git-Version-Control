Introduction:Peter Parker, also known as Spider-Man, is a fictional superhero created by writer Stan Lee and writer-artist Steve Ditko.

first appeared in Amazing Fantasy #15 in 1962.

Parker is a high school student who gains spider-like abilities after being bitten by a radioactive spider.

He uses his powers to fight crime and protect the city of New York as Spider-Man.
